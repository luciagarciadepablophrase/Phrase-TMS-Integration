# DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

**Between Cyera, Inc. ("Processor") and the Customer entity identified in the applicable Order Form ("Controller")**

**Effective Date:** As of the date of last signature below, or as incorporated by reference into the applicable Master Services Agreement.

---

## 1. Definitions

**"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person that the Controller submits to the Cyera platform for processing under the terms of the Master Services Agreement. **"Processing"** means any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, storage, analysis, classification, disclosure, or deletion. **"Security Incident"** means any confirmed unauthorized access to, or accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, or disclosure of, Personal Data processed under this Agreement.

---

## 2. Scope and Purpose of Processing

Cyera processes Personal Data solely to deliver the data security and cloud data posture management services described in the Order Form, and for no independent commercial purpose. The subject matter, duration, nature, and purpose of processing are set out in Annex I to this Agreement. Processing outside the scope defined in Annex I requires prior written authorization from the Controller.

---

## 3. Controller Instructions

Cyera shall process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller, including with regard to transfers of Personal Data to a third country, unless required to do so by applicable law. Where Cyera is required by law to process Personal Data beyond the Controller's documented instructions, Cyera shall inform the Controller of that legal requirement before processing, unless prohibited by law on grounds of public interest.

---

## 4. Confidentiality

Cyera ensures that all personnel authorized to process Personal Data are bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations and have received training commensurate with their access level. Access to Personal Data is restricted on a need-to-know basis and governed by Cyera's internal access control policies, which are reviewed no less than annually.

---

## 5. Security Measures

Cyera implements and maintains appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Personal Data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction, taking into account the state of the art and the nature of the data processed.

These measures include, at minimum:

- Encryption of Personal Data at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher)
- Role-based access control and multi-factor authentication for all platform access
- Continuous monitoring and anomaly detection across cloud data stores
- Annual penetration testing and vulnerability assessments by qualified third parties
- Documented incident response procedures with defined escalation paths

Cyera's security posture is validated under ISO 27001 certification and SOC 2 Type II attestation, copies of which are available to the Controller upon written request.

---

## 6. Sub-processors

Cyera shall not engage any new sub-processor to process Personal Data without first providing the Controller with at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice, during which the Controller may object on reasonable data protection grounds. The current list of approved sub-processors is maintained at cyera.io/legal/sub-processors and is updated upon any addition or replacement. Where Cyera engages sub-processors, it shall impose data protection obligations no less protective than those set out in this Agreement.

---

## 7. Data Subject Rights

Cyera shall, upon receipt of a written request from the Controller, provide reasonable assistance to enable the Controller to fulfill its obligations to respond to data subject requests, including requests for access, rectification, erasure, and portability, within the timelines required by applicable law.

---

## 8. Security Incident Notification

In the event of a confirmed Security Incident, Cyera shall notify the Controller without undue delay and, where feasible, no later than seventy-two (72) hours after becoming aware of the incident. Such notification shall include the nature of the incident, the categories and approximate number of data subjects affected, the categories of Personal Data involved, and the measures taken or proposed to address the incident. Cyera shall maintain and make available to the Controller upon request all records of Security Incidents, including root cause analysis and remediation actions taken.

---

## 9. Data Transfers

Where processing involves the transfer of Personal Data to a country outside the European Economic Area that has not received an adequacy decision from the European Commission, such transfer shall be governed by the Standard Contractual Clauses issued pursuant to Commission Decision 2021/914, incorporated herein by reference.

---

## 10. Return and Deletion of Data

Upon termination or expiry of the Master Services Agreement, Cyera shall, at the Controller's election, return or securely delete all Personal Data processed under this Agreement within thirty (30) days. Cyera ensures that all personnel authorized to process Personal Data are bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations and have received training commensurate with their access level.

---

## 11. Audits

The Controller, or an auditor mandated by the Controller under obligations of confidentiality, may conduct an audit of Cyera's processing activities no more than once per calendar year upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice, at the Controller's cost. Cyera shall provide reasonable cooperation with any such audit, including access to relevant documentation, facilities, and personnel.

---

## 12. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of law principles, except where superseded by applicable mandatory data protection law in the jurisdiction of the Controller.
